### Measurement And Acceptance

- Standard: **ITU-R BS.1770-5 (11/2023)**, channel-based whole-programme gated
  integrated loudness, measured after the actual selected channel mapping. Use
  LUFS for integrated loudness and dBTP for true peak; do not confuse sample peak,
  RMS, dialogue-gated loudness, or codec `dialnorm` with these measurements.
  LFE is excluded from programme loudness weighting but included in all-channel
  true-peak inspection. [ITU-R BS.1770-5](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I).
- Range: **EBU Tech 3342 (2023)** LRA, a gated short-term-loudness percentile
  descriptor, not sample dynamic range or compressor gain reduction. A range
  control of 7 or 11 is not a promise that FFmpeg's output will equal that number.
  Low range is accepted without adding dynamics. [EBU Tech 3342](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3342.pdf).
- Integrated acceptance: **target +/-0.2 LU on the normalized PCM checkpoint**;
  **target +/-0.5 LU on decoded lossy output**, inclusive. Decoded lossless/PCM
  output retains the PCM +/-0.2 LU, -2.8 dBTP, and A LRA-delta 0.5 LU limits;
  all outputs must also pass the preceding PCM checkpoint. No extra container
  tolerance. This universal acceptance proposal is measured only for AAC-LC,
  Opus, AC-3, and float PCM at **48,000 Hz** here. Select output rate through
  `preserve` or an exact positive integer Hz target; validate the realized rate
  against the selected codec/profile/layout and capability/compatibility snapshot.
  Never substitute 48 kHz for an explicit request.
- Peak processing budget: **-3.0 dBTP maximum**, not a target that every output
  must reach. PCM acceptance ceiling **-2.8 dBTP**; final decoded lossy ceiling
  **-1.0 dBTP**, inclusive. No extra +0.3 dB or codec overshoot allowance is added
  to the acceptance ceiling. A lower actual peak is valid. This headroom is a
  product margin, not a guarantee against codec overshoot.
- Measure the *decoded encoded file*, after resampling and encoding. No gain,
  compressor, limiter, implicit player-gain assumption, or other waveform change
  may follow final acceptance. Verification must be repeated on final materialized
  bytes under the existing replacement contract.
- For **3 <= duration < 60 seconds**, retain LRA diagnostically but use an
  inclusive maximum 3-second short-term loudness of **target +6 LU for B** and
  **target +5 LU for C**. A remains gain-only, without short-form range shaping.
  For duration **<3 seconds**, reject these normalization contracts as
  `audio.measurement_duration_insufficient`; preserve remains a separate requested
  policy, never a fallback. This short-form rule is a proposed product boundary.
  Only one fabricated 12-second B-above-cap predicate was tested, not real
  short-form normalization, C/equality/3-second/60-second boundaries, or the
  <3-second refusal. LRA is not recommended for short programmes in the
  broadcast guidance. [EBU R128 short-duration note](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128v4_0.pdf).
- Missing, duplicate, truncated, nonfinite, incompatible, or failed measurements
  reject the attempt. Digital silence is an explicit non-normalizable result,
  not zero LUFS; known clipped material is not declared repaired by normalization.
  Reject before native transformation for an unsupported preset/version, illegal
  codec rate/layout or unavailable required tool. After required source analysis
  but before transformation, reject positive anchor or A scalar gain above
  **18.0 dB**. This is not evidence of a cap on every instantaneous gain inside
  B/C's native normalizer. Do not silently clamp the request.
- Qualification must use a BS.1770/Tech3342-conformant meter and known-answer
  tests. The experiment's `loudnorm` and separate `ebur128` paths are **not two
  certified independent implementations**. Retained comparisons enforce <=0.2 LU
  integrated and <=0.3 dB true-peak disagreement at output. Recommend additionally
  requiring <=0.5 LU LRA disagreement during qualification at >=60 seconds.
  In `/2`, apply I/TP agreement at each required pre, PCM, and decoded-final
  checkpoint, and LRA agreement at those checkpoints where range is applicable.
  Disagreements hold qualification; do not pick the convenient meter. Use exact
  decimal comparisons for declared inclusive limits, not binary-float boundary
  drift. These proposed gates were not retroactively applied to old pass counts.

### Compressor And Order

Both compressed presets use FFmpeg `acompressor` with every meaningful control
explicit. Common values: `level_in=1`, `mode=downward`,
`threshold=0.06309573444801933` (linear amplitude for -24 dBFS),
`makeup=1` (0 dB), `knee=2.8284271247461903` (native knee-factor units),
`link=maximum`, `detection=rms`, `mix=1` (fully wet).

| Preset | Ratio | Attack | Release | Automatic makeup / additional gain |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| B | 2:1 | 20 ms | 250 ms | None |
| C | 3:1 | 10 ms | 150 ms | None |

Order, including all analysis identities:

1. Resolve the selected stream, channels/layout, and LFE policy; decode and render
   the actual target topology at the resolved target sample rate S. Neither pass may measure a different
   layout from the one being encoded. No new upmix or downmix matrix is selected
   by this investigation.
2. Measure that rendered source over the whole programme. For B/C, apply a
   constant input anchor gain of **-23 LUFS minus measured input I**, or
   **-26 LUFS minus measured input I for mono**. This anchors the absolute
   compressor threshold; it is not final loudness normalization. Positive gain
   over 18 dB fails. Process internally in floating point.
3. Apply the exact compressor above. Its maximum detector links all present
   channels, including every retained LFE channel, and applies the common gain. It does not claim
   to isolate speech or leave LFE samples unchanged. The retained 5.1 test is
   synthetic; LFE-driven pumping and speaker routing need human/package evidence.
4. For B/C, analyze the identical anchored/compressed chain in pass one. Record
   input I/TP/LRA/threshold and offset, then replay that identical chain with
   those values in pass two. `linear=true` may select either recorded native
   linear or dynamic normalization for B/C; both are explicitly allowed. Never
   assume the requested mode proves the executed mode.
5. Explicit final resampler:
   `aresample=S:resampler=swr:filter_size=32:phase_shift=10:exact_rational=1:dither_method=none`,
   where S is the compiled exact target Hz (48,000 in the retained experiment).
   Measure PCM, then encode using the chosen codec contract, then decode and
   measure again. No post-normalization makeup gain. No auto-retuning loop.
6. For A, replace steps 2-5's compressor/normalizer with **one constant measured
   gain `G = target I - measured input I`**, applied as
   `volume=GdB:precision=double`. Require measured input TP + G <= -3.0 dBTP and
   G <=18.0 dB first, then measure PCM and encoded output. No limiter or dynamic
   fallback is allowed. Input is already mapped and resampled to S before gain;
   a later quantization/codec stage still requires final decoded verification.
   The original attempted `loudnorm linear=true` adapter is rejected. The two-case
   scalar check is limited evidence for the proposed algorithm, not proof of
   equivalence to the old adapter or a production contract implementation.

### Codec And Bitrate Contract

Propose **`configurable-per-output-channel-rate/2`** as the universal meaning,
not a three-codec allowlist. A numeric nominal request N is an encoder allocation
in decimal bits/second **per realized output channel, including LFE**. It is not
a quality score, instantaneous cap, container bitrate, or required lower bound
on achieved payload. An optional independent maximum M limits the coded-packet
payload mean defined below. No +/-5% nominal window or implicit cap slack applies.

Exact admissible input and compilation contract:

1. `allocation` is explicitly `encoder-default` or
   `nominal-per-output-channel(N)`. `payload_mean_cap` is explicitly `none` or
   `maximum-per-output-channel(M)`. A cap-only request is allowed. Omitting a
   number never means zero and never permits discarding an explicit constraint.
   Encoder-default snapshots the selected codec's deterministic native
   default/quality/lossless mode; it is not a substitute for an unsupported N.
2. N and M are positive reduced rational bps/channel values with unsigned 64-bit
   numerator and denominator, denominator nonzero. Integer/decimal input is
   converted exactly; an explicit fraction supports native totals not evenly
   divisible by channel count. Reject zero, negatives, nonfinite input, ambiguous
   units, unsupported precision, or arithmetic overflow. When both are supplied,
   require M >= N. No arbitrary audible-quality minimum is invented.
3. Resolve actual output count C after the approved channel map and LFE policy,
   never from a requested minimum or source count. Compile N*C exactly to an
   unsigned 64-bit integer bps total accepted by the selected encoder/profile,
   sample rate, layout, and rate-control mode. Otherwise reject with a typed
   illegal-rate reason; never round, clamp, change mode, or pick another codec.
   Use checked widened arithmetic. M*C may remain an exact rational comparison
   bound. For example, a native total of 640,000 bps over six channels is
   N=320,000/3 bps/channel, not a rounded integer per-channel request. This is
   arithmetic illustration, not evidence of any codec/rate combination.
4. The legal-rate domain comes from a versioned, package-bound encoder adapter
   and capability snapshot: discrete legal sets for discrete-rate modes,
   documented bounds/granularity for others, and no fabricated nominal support
   for quality-only, lossless, PCM, or copy modes. Such modes remain available;
   they may use encoder-default and an explicit measurable cap. Reject only an
   actually incompatible numeric request, not the codec feature itself.
5. Preserve all baseline preferred/fallback codec selection, passthrough,
   channel/layout/minimum, LFE, surround, explicit downmix, and sample-rate
   controls. Rate is `preserve` or an exact positive integer Hz, resolved to S
   and verified exactly. An impossible codec/layout/rate combination fails
   visibly or uses only an already explicit configured fallback with a new
   compiled identity; never a hidden fallback from a failed measurement.
6. The domain is all available redistributable codecs/encoders and their legal
   channel/rate combinations under the full specification, capability and
   compatibility constraints. For normalization, require a known channel map
   with defined BS.1770 weights and measurable complete finite programme PCM of
   at least three seconds. No mono/stereo/5.1-only whitelist or 48-kHz-only gate
   is selected. Undefined topology/measurement is a named incompatible-request
   failure, not permission to guess a map or delete the wider feature.

The native legal domains and qualified measurement/metadata adapters still need
implementation and package evidence across the full included feature. Missing
evidence is a release work item, not a permanent exclusion disguised as a
capability failure. No universal codec quality or success guarantee is inferred.

The following **measured configurations only** instantiate N and M. They may be
offered as explicitly chosen convenience values after approval, never as the
only configurable values or automatic loudness-preset bitrate assignments.

No claim of a new codec, release container, or all player support follows.
HE-AAC, Vorbis, MP3, E-AC-3, and other unmeasured combinations remain included
where required/available under the specification; this matrix neither qualifies
nor excludes them. The full fixture matrix and target controls are still owed.
AC-3's unity `dialnorm` choice in the measured configuration is intentional
for already waveform-normalized consumer audio, not a truthful broadcast
dialogue-level deliverable; decoder DRC, metadata gain, and player behavior still
need release qualification. [FFmpeg codec options](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-codecs.html#libopus),
[AC-3 metadata](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-codecs.html#ac3-and-ac3_005ffixed).

For the cap, measure the selected encoded audio stream's **packet payload bits
divided by the sum of its positive packet durations expressed using the exact
stream time base**. Include all demuxed audio packets, including priming/padding
packets as represented by that packet timeline; exclude container overhead and
other streams. This is a coded-packet-time mean, not a presentation-time or
network-throughput claim. Retain numerator, denominator, packet count, total
payload, largest packet, time base, and skip/discard side data. Missing durations,
nonpositive sizes/durations, overflow, empty evidence, decode failure, or
unaccounted packets fail. A production implementation must also prove packet
coverage/timeline continuity rather than accepting incomplete probes.

When M is supplied, accept
**`8 * sum(payload_bytes) <= (M*C) * sum(packet_time)`** by exact rational
comparison. Equality passes; any positive excess, including one bit/second,
fails. With no M, there is no payload-mean cap to pretend was tested; all other
requested constraints and quality/replacement guards remain required. No
symmetric +/-5% nominal window and no implicit +1% maximum slack are selected.
The unapproved historical +/-5% rule is not modified or weakened in source here;
its replacement is explicitly part of the requested AUDIO approval. Codec-native
rate granularity is a compile-time legality check, not a verification exemption.
The fixed rows do **not** resolve all included configurable bitrate requests.
Existing ambiguous `audio_bitrate_bps` rows remain held until explicitly
mapped/replanned. A known legacy total requires an explicit semantics migration
and realized channel count before conversion to a per-channel rational; do not
reinterpret its stored integer as N. The new input shape, defaulting, nominal
meaning, optional cap, comparison domain, and legal-mode rejection are all part
of the proposed approval delta, not changes authorized by this report.

   defaults, resampler, and meters against BS.1770/Tech3342 known-answer material.
